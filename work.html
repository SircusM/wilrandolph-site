Industry Experience

Eight industries.
One discipline.

The ADP runs the same regardless of the industry. The operational inputs change. The strategic methodology does not. Nearly two decades of practice across sectors that span project-based business development, consumer brand management, luxury services, and healthcare-adjacent markets.

01

Consumer Goods & CPG

Brand governance, packaging strategy, seasonal campaign development, and new product category launches at global distribution scale. Documented 70% season-over-season revenue growth through packaging-led brand strategy.

02

Architecture, Engineering & Construction

Proposal strategy, business development marketing, firm positioning, and bid-winning collateral. The skills that win projects in AEC -- competitive positioning, audience intelligence, and differentiated narrative -- are the same skills that built this practice.

03

Luxury Real Estate

Enterprise brand architecture, agent personal brand systems, listing campaigns, and marketing strategy for a major luxury brokerage operating across three regional offices with nearly 200 agents.

04

Healthcare & Professional Services

Brand audits, competitive positioning, and marketing strategy for organizations that must communicate authority, trust, and clarity to sophisticated audiences. The diagnostic discipline that works in CPG works equally in healthcare.

05

Retail & Destination

Airport retailer destination packaging, specialty retail brand launches, and consumer-facing campaign systems. Market-specific positioning at scale across multiple geographic destinations simultaneously.

06

Fashion & Beauty

Brand identity development, editorial direction, campaign photography, and go-to-market strategy for independent labels and established brands. Full creative chain from strategy through final image.

07

Advertising & Print

Multi-channel campaign execution, trade show systems, editorial and publishing design, event marketing, and integrated print advertising across industries and audience segments.

08

Industrial Supply & B2B

Brand positioning and marketing strategy for organizations that sell to procurement professionals and sophisticated buyers. The ADP's operational intelligence layer is particularly suited to industries where the buyer understands the supply chain better than most marketers do.
